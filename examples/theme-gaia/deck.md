# The gaia theme

Warm paper, strong headings, a cover slide.

---

## Content slides

Gaia sets a heavy heading over a warm background, with a coloured band on the
left of ordinary slides.

- It reads well from the back of a room
- Bullets stay short
- One idea per slide

---

<!-- _class: lead invert -->

## Lead, inverted

The **lead** class centres a slide. Add **invert** for a dark one.

---

## Spot directives

A directive with an underscore styles **this slide only**. Without it, the
directive carries on to the slides after.

```markdown
<!-- _class: lead invert -->
# One slide

<!-- class: lead -->
# This one and the next ones
```
